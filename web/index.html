        00
        
At a glance

        
Headline numbers from the pipeline run. Everything on this page is read from the files in the selected
          results folder.

      

      

    


    

      

        01
        
Scene boundaries

        
Where the shot changes, found from sudden changes in colour content between neighbouring frames.

      

      

    


    

      

        02
        
Important frames

        
The sharpest and most representative frames of each scene, one to three per scene.

      

      

    


    

      

        03
        
Motion map

        
Object motion with the camera's own movement subtracted, so the hot spots are things that actually moved.

      

      

    


    

      

        04
        
Tracked objects

        
People, vehicles and animals followed through time. Counts are track IDs, not distinct individuals.

      

      

    


    

      

        05
        
Short summary

        
The highest-scoring moments, at least one per scene when the length allows, joined in time order.

      

      

    

  


  

    

      Built with OpenCV, PySceneDetect, YOLO11 and ByteTrack.
